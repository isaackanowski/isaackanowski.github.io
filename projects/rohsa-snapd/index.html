ROHSA-SNAPD

Spatially non-parametric recovery of intrinsic kinematic maps in pre- to post-merger galaxies

The majority of my PhD has involved developing and applying the kinematic deconvolution tool ROHSA-SNAPD, which I adapted from ROHSA by Antoine Marchal.

{\tt ROHSA-SNAPD} is designed to be easily applied to other 3D data and is agnostic to a specific emission line or wavelength.

I include here two example figures, the first is of an interacting galaxy observed by the KMOS3D survey and highlights the complex flux structure that the tool allows us to observe and analyse.

Figure 1. An interacting galaxy in the KMOS3D survey, as seen in 3-colour JWST imaging (left panel), observed H\(\alpha\) flux (middle panel) and deconvolved ROSHA-SNAPD flux (right panel).

The second figure shows the result of applying ROHSA-SNAPD to a mock observation of a kinematically complex (z=2) merger from the IllustrisTNG cosmological simulation in Kanowski et al. (2025). The deconvolved ROHSA-SNAPD kinematics agree well with the intrinsic simulation kinematics.

Figure 2. A mock observation of a \(z=2\) merger from the IllustrisTNG cosmological simulation (Kanowski et al. 2025).

The ROHSA-SNAPD code and an example application are available on the Github page.
