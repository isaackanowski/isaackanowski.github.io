Hi, I'm Isaac Kanowski, a final year PhD candidate at the [Research School of Astronomy & Astrophysics, Australian National University](https://rsaa.anu.edu.au/).
My research focuses on the star formation and dynamics of interacting galaxies at cosmic noon (z~1-2).

Throughout my PhD I have modelled and analysed integral field spectroscopic data from the [KMOS3D survey](https://www.mpe.mpg.de/ir/KMOS3D), identified interacting galaxies within large data sets (e.g. [3D-HST](https://archive.stsci.edu/prepds/3d-hst/), [CANDELS](https://archive.stsci.edu/hlsp/candels), [COSMOS-Web](https://cosmos.astro.caltech.edu/page/cosmosweb)) and developed a kinematic deconvolution tool called [ROHSA-SNAPD](https://github.com/isaackanowski/ROHSA_SNAPD) (it's publicly released if you would like to use it!).

In my Honours research I modelled strong gravitational lenses in the [MAGPI](https://magpisurvey.org/) VLT/MUSE survey to compare the total and stellar mass + dark matter measurements of elliptical galaxies.
